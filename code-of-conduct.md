# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, keep discussion on topic, and do not harass anyone. Credit creators for their work.

## Reporting

To report behavior that violates this code, contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/Li-Evan). Do not report violations in public issues. Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who repeatedly break them.
